- The boards are generated visual concepts, not editable Figma files or production-ready source code.
- Prices, loyalty points, commissions, targets, thresholds and example customer data shown in mockups are illustrative unless separately approved in the LUTIANO requirements.
- The Seller / Operations PWA is one app shared by three seller roles; the Customer PWA is a separate app.
